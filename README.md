## 🏙️ About Me 
I am a goal-driven BIM Specialist and Construction Engineer proficient in BIM tools such as Autodesk Revit, Navisworks, Forma, BIM360, and Hexagon CADWorx, integrating different trades like architecture, structural, and MEPFS into a centralized model. 

I pair BIM expertise with field construction experience to produce precise, constructible models that reflect how projects are actually built.

I aspire to be an architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) educator dedicated to preparing trainees and students in the academe to keep the Philippines competitive in the global industry market.
